1. Introduction

Welcome to Voltcore ("we", "our", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile game. Voltcore is developed by Burak Erol.

2. Information We Collect

Voltcore is designed to respect your privacy. We collect NO personal information.

Data Stored Locally on Your Device:

  • Your game progress (completed levels, stars earned, scores)
  • Your settings (sound, music, language, controls preference)
  • Your local leaderboard records

All this data is stored only on your device using the device's local storage. It is never transmitted to our servers or any third party.

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • ❌ No personal identifying information (name, email, phone)
  • ❌ No location data
  • ❌ No advertising identifiers
  • ❌ No analytics or tracking data
  • ❌ No payment information (game is completely free)
  • ❌ No social media integration
  • ❌ No camera, microphone, or contacts access

4. Third-Party Services

Voltcore does not use any third-party services, including:

  • No advertising networks (AdMob, Facebook Audience Network, etc.)
  • No analytics services (Firebase, Google Analytics, etc.)
  • No social platforms (Game Center, Facebook, etc.)
  • No cloud save services

5. Children's Privacy

Voltcore is safe for all ages, including children under 13. We do not collect any personal information from users of any age. The game contains no violence, no inappropriate content, and no advertising.

6. Data Security

Since we don't collect or transmit any data, there is no risk of data breach affecting your information. Your game data lives only on your device and you can delete it anytime by uninstalling the app.

7. Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • Delete: Uninstall the app to remove all local data
  • Reset: Use the in-game reset option (if available) to clear progress
  • Access: All data is on your device, accessible to you

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
